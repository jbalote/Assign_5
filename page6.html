Page 6: Understanding Slope

Meaning of Slope

Slope describes how fast y changes for each 1-unit change in x.

Slope Formula

m = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1) from two points.

Positive vs Negative

Positive slope rises left to right; negative slope falls left to right.

Lecture Notes

  • Slope compares vertical change (rise) to horizontal change (run).
  • Undefined slope happens when run is zero, which creates a vertical line.
  • Zero slope means no vertical change, producing a horizontal line.
From (1,2) to (3,6), slope = (6-2)/(3-1) = 2
