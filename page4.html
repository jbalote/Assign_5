Page 4: Solving Two-Step Equations

Step Order

Undo addition/subtraction first, then multiplication/division.

Keep It Balanced

Whatever you do on one side, do the same on the other side.

Write Clearly

Show each step on a new line to avoid mistakes.

Lecture Notes

  • For two-step equations, clear constants before dividing by the coefficient.
  • Writing one algebra move per line reduces sign mistakes.
  • If fractions appear, multiply both sides by a common denominator to simplify.
