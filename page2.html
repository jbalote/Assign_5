Page 2: What Is a Linear Equation?

Definition

A linear equation is an equation where the variable has exponent 1 and the graph is a straight line.

Standard Form

One common form is Ax + By = C, where A, B, and C are constants.

Simple Example

3x + 2 = 14 is linear because x appears only to the first power.

Lecture Notes

  • A linear equation keeps variables to the first power only.
  • You can rewrite equations into different forms without changing their meaning.
  • When graphed, all valid solutions lie on a straight line.
Linear equations model constant change.
