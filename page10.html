Page 10: Quick Review and Practice

Key Ideas

  • Linear equations have variable power 1.
  • Linear functions follow y = mx + b.
  • Slope is the rate of change.

Practice 1

Solve: 4x - 8 = 16

Answer: x = 6

Practice 2

Find slope between points (2,5) and (6,13).

Answer: m = 2
