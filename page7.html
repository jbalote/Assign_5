Page 7: Graphing Linear Functions

Find y-Intercept

In y = mx + b, the intercept is the point (0, b).

Use Slope

From the intercept, move rise over run to find another point.

Draw the Line

Connect points with a straight line and extend in both directions.

Lecture Notes

  • Plot the y-intercept first because it is given directly by b.
  • Use slope direction carefully: negative slope means down as you move right.
  • Two precise points are enough to draw a linear graph.
